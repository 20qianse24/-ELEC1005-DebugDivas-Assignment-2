# ELEC1005-DebugDivas-Assignment-2
Assignment 2 ELEC1005
Project Overview: Unified Communications and team management platform for the NSW State Emergency Service to organise spntaneous volunteer allocation to unexpected emergencies.

Targeted Problem: Current primary communication channels with volunteer rescuers are scattered across social media platforms, resulting in unreliable response times and coordination of volunteers and resources. A unified platform keeps all communications and information on emergencies and hazards in one central location and easily accessible.

Users: Made for Volunteers and Duty Officers involved in responding to unexpected emergencies

Key features:
- Duty Officers:
  - Register new emergencies
  - View worked hours of volunteers to prevent overexertion
  - Mark emergencies as complete
- Volunteers AND Duty Officers:
  - Report hazards at emergency sites
  - Communicate directly with team members via a groupchat
  - Recieve and accept/decline assigments
  - View relevant information on emergencies and hazards
